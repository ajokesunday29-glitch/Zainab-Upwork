Frequently asked questions

Everything brands usually ask before starting

What exactly is a "faceless UGC video ad"?
A faceless UGC ad is a short, native-feeling ad — product-in-hand demos, AI avatar presenters, or voiceover-led content — that looks and feels like organic TikTok or Reels content, without a hired creator appearing on camera.
Do I need to ship you my physical product?
No. Because these are faceless, AI-assisted and stock/b-roll-supported productions, I don't require you to mail a physical unit. If you'd like a specific real-product shot included, we can discuss what's possible for your item.
Which platforms do you create ads for?
I create and format ads specifically for Shopify stores, TikTok Shop and Meta (Facebook & Instagram) ads. Every deliverable is exported in the aspect ratio and length each platform performs best with.
Do you write the hook and script, or do I need to provide one?
I write the hook and full script as part of every project, based on your product, offer and target audience. If you already have a script or angle you want used, I'll work from that instead.
How long does a typical ad take to deliver?
A first draft is typically ready in 48–72 hours after the brief and hook are approved. Batches of multiple ads or custom production may take longer, and I'll confirm a timeline before starting.
How many revisions are included?
Starter packages include 1 round of revisions and Growth packages include 2, covering edits to pacing, captions, music and minor script tweaks. Full script rewrites or reshoots outside the original brief are scoped separately.
Can you make multiple ad variations for A/B testing?
Yes — this is the core of the Growth package. I can produce several hook or angle variations of the same underlying ad so you can test which performs best in your ad account.
Do you use AI avatars, and do they look realistic?
Yes, AI avatars and AI voiceover are one of the faceless formats I offer. I'll show examples and discuss whether an avatar, voiceover-only, or product-in-hand format is the best fit for your brand before production starts.
What file formats and lengths do you deliver?
Ads are delivered as vertical (9:16) MP4 files by default, sized for TikTok, Reels and Shorts placements, typically 15–60 seconds long. Square or horizontal versions can be added for Meta feed placements on request.
Do you sign NDAs or product confidentiality agreements?
Yes. I'm comfortable signing an NDA for brands who need one, especially for unreleased products or exclusive offers.
What's your pricing and how is a project scoped?
My base rate is $15/hr. Most clients prefer a flat package (see Pricing above) scoped after a short brief call or message, so you know the full cost upfront before work begins.
Do you offer ongoing monthly ad creative?
Yes — the Scale package is built for brands that want a steady stream of new hooks and ad variations every week or two, rather than one-off projects.
How do we get started?
Message me on WhatsApp or email with your product link, target platform and any past-performing ads. I'll reply with a scoped package and timeline within one business day.
